📊 ANALYZE TAB

Enter any US stock ticker (e.g. AAPL, NVDA) and press ANALYZE. The app fetches data from up to 6 sources and scores the stock on 91 points: 48 Fundamental + 43 Technical. A red STOP button appears while analysis is running — tap it to cancel a stuck or unwanted analysis in progress.

  • Score ≥80 = STRONG setup — high conviction entry
  • Score 65–79 = POSSIBLE — confirm on chart before entry
  • Score 45–64 = MONITOR — wait for improvement
  • Score <45 = AVOID
⚖ SCORING MODE — BALANCED / STRICT QUALITY / AGGRESSIVE QUALITY

These 3 buttons change how strict the fundamental thresholds are before scoring the same raw data — technical scoring, entry prices, and stop logic never change between modes.

  • Balanced (default) — wider thresholds, works well for growth stocks and volatile sectors where classic "value" ratios run hot
  • Strict Quality — tighter thresholds, built for high-quality value & dividend stocks; a mediocre stock will score lower here than in Balanced
  • Aggressive Quality — relaxes only 4 fundamental checks (D/E, ROE, P/E, Gross Margin) beyond Balanced; useful for early-stage growth names still burning cash

Switching modes and re-running ANALYZE on the same ticker can change the score — that's expected, not a bug; you're asking a different question ("is this good by strict value standards?" vs "is this good for an aggressive growth bet?").

↻ REFRESH PRICE / ⏱ AUTO-REFRESH (30s)

Refresh Price re-fetches only the current price (fast, minimal API usage) without re-running the full 91-point analysis — use it to check if price has moved since you last analyzed. Auto-Refresh (30s) toggles this on a repeating 30-second timer; turn it off when you're done watching a ticker to avoid burning API quota unnecessarily.

📰 NEWS & SENTIMENT / W · D CHART TOGGLE

Below the entry scenarios, the News & Sentiment panel shows recent headlines and the next/last earnings date for the ticker, plus its Relative Strength vs SPY/QQQ — is this stock outperforming or lagging the broader market right now? The live chart's W / D buttons switch between Weekly and Daily candles — Weekly for the big trend, Daily for precise entry timing.

🔔 ENTRY PARAMETERS vs 🎯 IDEAL SETUP

Entry Parameters = The entry price TODAY based on current price position vs MA50, Fibonacci levels, and ATR. This is the practical, actionable entry with Stop Loss and 3 Take Profit levels.

Ideal Setup = The optimal pullback zone — typically Fibonacci 38.2–50% or MA50. Waiting for price to reach here gives the best Risk/Reward. Not every stock will pull back this far.

📡 WATCHLIST TAB

After analyzing a stock, add it to the Watchlist. You'll choose which of the 4 entry scenarios to track — Today, Short-Term, Medium-Term, Ideal Entry — each with its own Entry, Stop, and TP1/TP2/TP3. You can track more than one scenario for the same stock at once.

  • Every 60 seconds, the app checks live prices for everything you're tracking
  • NEAR — price is within 1.5% of your Entry. You'll get a notification, but the app never enters a trade for you — that decision is always yours (tap "Mark as Entered")
  • TP1 hit → suggested: sell 30%, Stop auto-moves to Break Even (your entry price) — you can no longer lose money on this position
  • TP2 hit → suggested: sell 40% more, Stop auto-moves up to TP1 — locks in more profit
  • TP3 hit → full target reached, position closes, P&L is recorded
  • If price falls below the current Stop at any stage → Stopped Out, P&L recorded, and the alert resets so you'll be notified again if price returns to the original Entry zone
  • Alerts fire as browser push notifications + Telegram (if configured) — see the iOS note below

↻ Refresh All — re-fetches live prices one ticker at a time for every tracked stock (with a short pause between each, to be gentle on your API quota); the button turns into ■ Stop Refresh while running — tap it again to cancel partway through a long list. 📒 Journal — a running log of every closed position (stopped out or hit TP3), with entry/exit price and recorded P&L, so you can review your actual trading history over time. 📋 Export CSV — downloads your full Watchlist as a spreadsheet file for your own records or tax purposes. 🗑 Clear All — removes every tracked stock and alert from the Watchlist; this cannot be undone, so the app will ask you to confirm first.

🔔 ALERTS TAB

Create custom price alerts, independent of the Watchlist's entry/stop tracking above. Pick a type, enter a ticker and price(s), then tap + ADD:

  • ▲ Above — notify when price rises above a level
  • ▼ Below — notify when price falls below a level
  • 🎯 Target — notify once price touches an exact level
  • 📍 Zone — notify when price enters a range between two levels

The app checks every 60 seconds while open and fires browser notifications. For background alerts (app closed), use Telegram via Settings. 🔔 Enable Push Notifications requests your browser's permission to show alerts (required once per device/browser). ✕ Clear Badge resets the unread-alert counter shown on the tab. ✕ Disable Triggered Alerts — despite the name, this permanently deletes every alert that has already fired (not just hides them) — there's no confirmation prompt, so use it only when you're sure you don't want that history anymore.

⚙️ SETTINGS TAB

Enter API keys (Finnhub, Twelve Data, FMP, Polygon, Alpha Vantage, Anthropic). At least one key required. Twelve Data is recommended for RSI + MA. Configure Telegram bot for background alerts.

📈 DAILY API QUOTA PANEL

Shows how much of each provider's free daily allowance you've used today (e.g. FMP: 42/250), resetting at midnight UTC — not your local midnight, since that's when the providers themselves reset. If a number turns orange/red, that source is close to or past its daily limit and the app will automatically skip it for the rest of the day rather than sending calls that would just fail.

📡 DATA SOURCES — WHY NUMBERS CAN CHANGE BETWEEN RUNS

This app pulls data from up to 6 providers (Finnhub, Twelve Data, FMP, Polygon, Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage) and uses whichever responds first for each field. If a provider is slow, rate-limited, or down at the moment you run an analysis, the app automatically falls back to the next one — so the same stock can show a slightly different ROE, Gross Margin, or P/E if you re-run it later, even on the same day.

This is normal, not a bug — different providers report figures using slightly different methodologies (e.g. TTM vs. last fiscal year) and update on different schedules. Wherever you see a source tag like (FH), (YF), (AV), or (FMP) next to a number, that tells you which provider it came from for that specific run.

The DATA SOURCES percentage on the score screen is not a measure of accuracy — it simply counts how many of the 6 providers responded at all during that analysis. A lower percentage means fewer sources were reachable (e.g. a slow network or a daily quota limit), not that the numbers shown are less correct.

Two yellow banners can appear above a result: "FMP daily quota (250 req) reached" means FMP's real 250/day limit is genuinely hit (check the quota panel above to confirm) — "FMP request failed" means something else went wrong with FMP specifically (invalid/expired key, or a plan that doesn't include that endpoint) while your daily quota is still far from 250. Either way the app automatically continues using Finnhub instead. A "PARTIAL DATA — score may be understated" box lists any individual field (e.g. FCF) that came back empty from every source that was tried for it — the score itself is still valid, just possibly a little lower than it would be with that one field included.

📐 R/R — RISK/REWARD RATIO

R/R of 1:2 means: risk $1 to make $2. TP1 is the first realistic target. Stop Loss is the maximum loss if wrong. R/R ≥1:2 is recommended. R/R below 1:1.5 is flagged as poor.

🧮 POSITION SIZE CALCULATOR & THE IRON RULE

Found below the entry scenarios on the Analyze screen. Enter your Account Size, Entry Price, and Stop Loss — the calculator tells you exactly how many shares to buy so that if the stop is hit, you lose a fixed, known percentage of your account, never more. Use the TODAY / SHORT / MED / IDEAL buttons to auto-load Entry and Stop from whichever of the three entry scenarios you're considering.

⚖ The Iron Rule: never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single trade (absolute hard max 3%). If a stop is wide (far from entry), the calculator will tell you to buy fewer shares — shrink the position instead of accepting more risk per trade. This rule is fixed and does not change based on how confident you feel about a setup.

Allow fractional shares (off by default) — when your broker supports buying partial shares (most modern brokers do), turn this on to size a position by the exact dollar amount instead of rounding down to whole shares. This matters most for a small account or an expensive stock: without it, a $50 account trying to buy a $500 stock always shows "0 shares" and no valid position, because there's no whole share it can afford within the risk budget — with it, the same account can still use its full 1–2% risk allowance by buying e.g. 0.05 shares.
